4. Trust notes

Synthetic data and subjective weights limit trust. TOPSIS and weighted sum are compared above for method agreement; AHP consistency ratio below 0.10 is acceptable. The system supports the manager’s decision and does not replace it. Any sensitive supplier data should be anonymised before real use.
